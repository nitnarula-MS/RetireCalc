Expenses & assumptions

Withdrawal order: RRSP first, then Cash, then TFSA last. Every year from retirement until the RRSP mandatory age above, an extra amount is pulled from RRSP beyond what's needed for spending and moved into TFSA, up to the per-person annual limit — shifting money out of taxable RRSP room into tax-free TFSA room while it's still available. TFSA and Cash withdrawals are never taxed; once the RRSP is fully drawn down, only CPP and OAS (after any clawback) remain taxable.
